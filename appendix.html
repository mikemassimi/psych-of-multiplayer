Beyond social psychology

The breadth check

We looked across the whole discipline, not just the CSCW-adjacent corner. Each subfield of psychology has something to say about multiplayer AI.

SubfieldCore mechanismMultiplayer-AI implication
CognitiveWorking memory, joint attentionAgents can add or absorb cognitive load depending on design
SocialLoafing, bystander, identity, faultlinesThe deepest-covered subfield — see the 13 frameworks
DevelopmentalAttachment, parasocial bonds, scaffoldingUneven human-agent bonding creates within-channel faultlines
EmotionalEmotional contagion, affect-as-informationAgent tone is a channel-wide emotional-climate lever
OrganizationalPsychological safety, role clarityAgents can silently erode safety to ask, admit, and try
EvolutionaryDunbar's number, coalition trackingAgent-dense channels may exceed cognitive group-size limits
PersonalityIndividual differences in engagementExpect and design for variance, not an averaged "the user"
Cross-culturalIndividualism/collectivism, power distanceTrust and deference to agents likely varies by region
ClinicalTechnostressGives a measurable wellbeing cost metric, not just productivity
